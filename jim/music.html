Miscellaneous 

Last Updated: Thu May 21 10:01:20 BST 2009


This page is collection of some of the music tracks been involved with. I recently got a Lexicon Omega and have been using it to capture some 4-track recordings into GarageBand and re-mix them.

I've only just started using GarageBand, so I'll probably do several mixes of each song until I get it right. So these files will probably be replaced with newer versions as I improve my competence.

  • Another Girl, Another Planet [MP3, 3.76MB]
    Taken from the original Macaroni Penguins demo session (hence the tape hiss). I've redubbed the bass (to get rid of Fred (sorry)), separated out the solos so that they can overlap the vocals and remixed it in GarageBand.
    Song: The Only Ones
    Performance: Macaroni Penguins
    Mix: 13th June 2005 (headphones)

  • Psycho Killer [MP3, 5.41MB]
    Another track taken from the original Macaroni Penguins demo session (more hiss) - although we never mixed down this track originally. Another re-dubbed bass line (another apology to Fred), and remix.
    Song: Talking Heads
    Performance: Macaroni Penguins
    Mix: 9th Jul 2005 (Hi-Fi)

  • Tell It Like It Is [MP3, 4.40MB]
    Written as a song for Macaroni Penguins, (as part of the Tell It Like It Isn't demo) although we've yet to play it at a gig. This is a pretty straight mix from the master - with a bit of a whoosh added to the end of the guitar solo, as I'm freed from having to share it's track with the vocals.
    Song: Jim Randell, Mark Bailey
    Performance: Jim Randell, Mark Bailey
    Mix: 13th June 2005 (speakers)

  • Messing Around (Shoot Me Down In Flames) [MP3, 5.30MB]
    A song that developed out of a warm-up for Macaroni Penguins at Gig 31. This is a pretty straight mix from the master (which was recorded live with a drum machine (which we couldn't hear very well in our headphones, so we drift a bit at one point)), with Mark doing his own backing vocals, thanks to a bit of GarageBand jiggery pokery.
    Song: Macaroni Penguins
    Performance: Jim Randell, Mark Bailey
    Mix: 3rd June 2005 (speakers)

  • There's Nobody Else [MP3, 4.18MB]
    Another song written for Macaroni Penguins, as part of the Tell It Like It Isn't demo.
    Song: Jim Randell, Mark Bailey
    Performance: Jim Randell, Mark Bailey
    Mix: 25th June 2005 (headphones)

  • This [MP3, 5.00MB]
    Another song written for Macaroni Penguins, as part of the Tell It Like It Isn't demo. Featuring my first use of GarageBand "Software Instruments" (for the keyboard parts - which were formerly added manually at mix-down).
    Song: Jim Randell, Mark Bailey
    Performance: Jim Randell, Mark Bailey
    Mix: 5th June 2005 (headphones)

  • The Future's So Bright I Gotta Wear Shades [MP3, 3.50MB]
    Another song taken from the Macaroni Penguins original demo session, but never mixed down before. Again, I've redubbed the bass (to get rid of Fred).
    Song: Timbuck 3
    Performance: Macaroni Penguins
    Mix: 24th July 2005 (Hi-Fi)

  • Another Girl, Another Planet [MP3, 3.69MB]
    In preparation for our Cam FM session we ran through a few songs in an unplugged style. This is the result all recorded live, straight into GarageBand.
    Song: The Only Ones
    Performance: Macaroni Penguins
    Mix: 24th July 2005 (Hi-Fi)
