Jim's High Mountain Walks

This page is a list of walks that I've done on the High Mountains of Britain and Ireland. My plan is to complete them all before my fortieth birthday, see my "campaign" page.

Some of the distances are estimated (or absent) because I either couldn't remember the exact route, or I haven't got round to filling it all in yet. Likewise the comments will be padded out when I've got the time.
