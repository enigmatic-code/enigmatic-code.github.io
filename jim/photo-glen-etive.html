On 20-21 March 1999, myself and four other intrepid explorers ventured into the wilds of mountains above Glen Etive. We survived a wet day and spent the night camping on a high col in atrocious weather to see in improved weather on the first day of Spring.

This is a chronological list of the photos taken on the second day.

4. 21st March 1999 Glas Beinn Mhor and Ben Starav from Stob Coir' an Albannaich

7. 21st March 1999 Simon descending a snow slope on Stob Coir an Albannaich
8. 21st March 1999 Avalanche debris below summit of Stob Coir an Albannaich
9. 21st March 1999 Stob Coir' an Albannaich from Meall Tarsuinn
