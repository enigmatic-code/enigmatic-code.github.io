12th June 1994 Wintour's Leap, Wye Valley
Left Hand Route [var] 290ft HS (5a,4a,-,-) lead, second: NC, DR
Due to route finding error I lead up an HVS (Gryke) passing the crux before I realised my error. I traversed back onto the route and continued to the stance. Nick couldn't follow the 4b crux of the proper line, so I abseiled down, top roped the first part of the pitch and set up a stance just above the crux to talk the others through it.
