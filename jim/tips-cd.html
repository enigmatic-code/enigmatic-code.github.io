CD-ROM Tips

Platform: Linux

Mounting a CD-ROM with Case Insensitive filenames

Mount the CD-ROM with the following command: 

mount -t iso9660 -o ro,check=relaxed /dev/cdrom /mnt/cdrom

You may have to do this as root, even though you can normally mount CD-ROMs as a non-root user.
