Jim's Final Munro

On 19th May 2007 I ascended Ben Chonzie (3048ft) and completed my ascent of the 3000ft mountains in Britain and Ireland (there are 313, and I started nearly 20 years ago). Although the weather was foul 9 friends joined me on the summit.
L to R: Mark Bailey, Simon Love (Munroist #1444), Graeme Tough, Ruth Love (Munroist #1443), Jim Randell (Munroist #3806), Lindsay Gough, Dave Gee, Dave Randell, Sam Dickinson, Duncan Gough.
