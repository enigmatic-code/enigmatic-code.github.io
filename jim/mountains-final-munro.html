Jim's Final Munro

I have finished the Munros ...

19 years, 7 months and 21 days after I first reached the summit of Cairn Gorm I completed my round of the Munros, and became a Munroist, on 19th May 2007 when I reached the summit of Ben Chonzie. The weather was foul, but 9 of my friends joined me on the summit to celebrate.

I have been assigned Munroist Number #3806 by the SMC.

Some Statistics

These figures are for walks to bag the 3000ft peaks of Britain and Ireland, so they include all peaks in Scotland, Wales, Ireland and England.

  • First Munro: Cairn Gorm, 28th September 1987
  • Final Munro: Ben Chonzie, 19th May 2007
  • Elapsed Time: 19 years, 7 months, 21 days
  • Total Number of Walks: 148 (over 169 days, on 41 trips)
  • Total Distance Walked: 1788 miles
  • Total Number of Peaks Ascended: 331 (18 reascents, plus around 128 tops)
  • Number of Peaks Ascended Solo: 16 (over 11 walks)
  • Pairs of Boots Used: 6 (of which 2 are still servicable)
